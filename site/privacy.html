Privacy / October 18, 2026

Your privacy.

This website does not use analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, or newsletter subscriptions. The inquiry form sends your name, email address, inquiry type, optional business name, and message through Web3Forms for delivery to Lemun at sales@lemundrinks.com. This website does not save your inquiry in browser storage.

Contacting Lemun

If you send an email, text, or call, Lemun receives the information you choose to share so it can respond to your inquiry. Avoid sending sensitive personal or medical information. For questions about information shared with Lemun, contact sales@lemundrinks.com.

Form submissions

Web3Forms processes and stores submissions according to its retention settings and may share submitter IP and email addresses with its spam-prevention providers. See Web3Forms’ privacy policy. Lemun uses your inquiry to respond to your request. You may also contact Lemun directly by email or phone.

External services

Directions open Google Maps. Social links open Instagram or TikTok. Those services apply their own privacy practices. A website hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses in service logs.
